King Of Bollywood

- Salman Khan

Salman Khan is one of Bollywood's most influential and commercially successful megastars, with an acting, producing, and hosting career spanning over three decades. Born on December 27, 1965, into a prominent cinematic family as the eldest son of legendary screenwriter Salim Khan, he achieved instant stardom with the iconic romantic blockbuster Maine Pyar Kiya (1989).Since then, he has consistently dominated the Indian box office, establishing himself as a premier action and romantic hero through major record-breaking blockbusters such as Hum Aapke Hain Koun..!, Dabangg, Sultan, Bajrangi Bhaijaan, and the highly successful Tiger franchise. Affectionately referred to as "Bhai" by his massive, dedicated global fanbase, his immense cultural popularity extends directly to Indian television, where he has hosted the widely popular reality show Bigg Boss for over a decade.Beyond his cinematic triumphs and high-profile media controversies, he is widely recognized for his extensive humanitarian efforts. Through his non-profit organization, the Being Human Foundation, he actively funds vital healthcare and education initiatives for underprivileged communities across India, solidifying his status as an enduring icon whose vast influence reaches far automotive beyond the realms of commercial entertainment.
